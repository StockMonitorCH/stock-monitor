Privacy Policy

Chart Monitor · Last updated: June 2026

📊 Data Storage

Chart Monitor stores all user data (watchlist, notes, indicator settings, price alerts) exclusively on your device. No personal data or usage statistics are transmitted to any server.

📈 Market Data

Stock prices, exchange rates and financial data are fetched in real time from Yahoo Finance. Chart Monitor acts as a client only — no data entered by the user is sent to Yahoo Finance.

Yahoo Finance privacy policy: legal.yahoo.com

Stock screener data (pre-computed financial metrics such as P/E ratio, market capitalisation, and sector) is loaded from stock-monitor.ch. This is a read-only request; no user data is transmitted.

🔔 Notifications

Price alerts (Stop-Loss / Target Price) are processed entirely on your device using Android WorkManager. No alert data is transmitted to external servers.

🔄 Update Check

The in-app update check connects to GitHub Releases to compare version numbers. No personal data is transmitted during this process.

GitHub privacy policy: docs.github.com

📋 No Tracking

Chart Monitor contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app is free and open source (GPL-3.0).

✉ Contact

Questions regarding privacy can be directed to: info@stock-monitor.ch
