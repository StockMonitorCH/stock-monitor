FX Calc – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data collection

FX Calc does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No user accounts, email addresses, location data, or any other personal information is gathered.

Exchange rates

Live exchange rates are fetched directly from Yahoo Finance (read-only). No user-related data is transmitted — only a request for rate data is made.

Local storage

All app settings (e.g. preferred currency pairs) are stored exclusively on the user's device. No synchronisation with external servers takes place.

Third parties

FX Calc uses no analytics tools, no advertising, no tracking, and no social media integrations.

Contact

For privacy-related questions: info@stock-monitor.ch
